## Front matter

Every report needs a title, a one-sentence description (it becomes the search snippet and the share text), a date, and a grade on the Swiss hiking scale. T1 is a stroll: a short note. T6 is an alpine route: long, technical, figures throughout. Reading time is worked out from the text, and the masthead profile is drawn from how dense each stretch of writing is.

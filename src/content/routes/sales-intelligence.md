A lot of winning work happens long before a bid. Knowing which client to visit, who to take with you, and what to bring up usually lives in people's heads. This tool reads the client relationships, open opportunities, what they're worth and how past bids went, and suggests a handful of concrete actions. For example: take a particular colleague to a client meeting, because they delivered a similar project there two years ago, and raise these three points.

Hundreds of infrastructure projects lived in spreadsheets, and the utility demand forecasts in a long PDF. Now they're on one map. You can drag a project to reschedule it, colour the whole programme by any attribute, and move climate and population sliders to see where supply falls short. Every number traces back to its source, and the default settings match the official baseline exactly.

Engineers and project managers spend a lot of time looking things up: a clause in a contract, a figure in a design report, what changed between two long revisions. This started about a year ago as a chatbot over a document library. Now people use it to find answers they can cite, and to turn them into deliverables: reports, filled-in pricing sheets, slide decks, and lately interactive decks that can go straight to a client.

Most of the work has gone into making the answers reliable on messy documents. Every change gets replayed against real questions people asked, and scored by judges that don't know which version wrote the answer.
